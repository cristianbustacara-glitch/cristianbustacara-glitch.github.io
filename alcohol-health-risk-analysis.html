Alcohol & Health Risk Analysis

An interactive Power BI report that brings together World Health Organization indicators on alcohol consumption, heavy episodic drinking, and alcohol-attributable mortality.

Explore the dashboardHow it was built

The problem

The WHO publishes separate indicators for drinking patterns and alcohol-related harm. Comparing countries and demographic groups across those indicators requires consistent definitions and a clear way to keep the measures separate.

The solution

I prepared the WHO data in Power Query, built a model in Power BI, and used DAX to calculate indicators for comparison. The Overview summarizes the main metrics, Mortality focuses on alcohol-attributable deaths, and Risk – HED explores heavy episodic drinking across countries and by sex.

Tools · Power BI, Power Query, DAX, data modeling
