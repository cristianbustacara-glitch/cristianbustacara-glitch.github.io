The problem

HR needed a consistent way to collect feedback in both directions and review it in one place. Each evaluation needed to identify who gave and received the feedback, use the same scoring criteria, and document the reasoning behind the ratings.

The solution

I built a Power Apps form that records ratings for goal achievement, quality of work, communication, and teamwork, with a required written comment of at least 30 characters. SharePoint Lists store employee profiles and evaluations. In Power BI, I built an overview for coverage and score distribution and an individual view for ratings by criterion, comments, and evaluation history.

Tools · Power Apps (Power Fx), SharePoint Lists, Power BI (Power Query and DAX).
